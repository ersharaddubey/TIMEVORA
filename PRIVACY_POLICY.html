Welcome to TIMEVORA. Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how TIMEVORA ("we", "our", or "us") collects, uses, stores, and protects information when you use our mobile application and related services.

By using TIMEVORA, you acknowledge that you have read this Privacy Policy.

1. About TIMEVORA

TIMEVORA is a productivity and time-management application that provides features such as focus sessions, Pomodoro timers, task planning, habit tracking, diary, reports, usage tracking, widgets, reminders, and other productivity tools.

2. Information We May Collect

Depending on the features you use and the permissions you grant, TIMEVORA may process the following categories of information:

  • Productivity Information: Tasks, planner entries, focus sessions, Pomodoro records, habits, diary entries, goals, streaks, and related activity.
  • App Usage Information: App usage statistics and activity information when you enable the usage tracking feature and grant the required Android permission.
  • Preferences: Theme settings, notification preferences, widget settings, and other application preferences.
  • Device Information: Certain technical information required for application functionality, diagnostics, security, and compatibility.
  • Sync and Social Features: Information required to operate synchronization, together features, or friend challenges when you choose to use them.

The exact information processed depends on the features you use, your settings, and the permissions you grant.

3. How We Use Information

Information may be used to:

  • Provide and maintain TIMEVORA features.
  • Save and manage your tasks, habits, focus sessions, and productivity records.
  • Generate productivity reports and usage summaries.
  • Provide reminders, notifications, and widgets.
  • Enable synchronization and collaboration features where available.
  • Improve application performance, reliability, and security.
  • Respond to support requests and comply with legal obligations.

4. Android Permissions

TIMEVORA may request certain Android permissions depending on the features you choose to use.

  • Usage Access: If enabled, this permission allows TIMEVORA to access app usage statistics for its usage tracking and productivity features.
  • Notifications: Used to deliver reminders, focus-session alerts, and other notifications.
  • Background Activity: Certain background operations may be required to maintain timers, reminders, synchronization, or related functionality.
  • Internet Access: May be used for online services, synchronization, or other connected features.

Permissions are subject to Android operating-system controls. You can manage or revoke permissions through your device settings, although some features may stop working as a result.

5. Data Storage and Security

TIMEVORA may store application information locally on your device. Where online synchronization or connected services are enabled, relevant information may also be processed or stored by our servers or service providers.

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

6. Data Sharing and Third-Party Services

We do not sell your personal information.

Information may be shared only when reasonably necessary to:

  • Operate application features and connected services.
  • Provide synchronization or collaboration features that you choose to use.
  • Work with service providers who help operate or maintain the application.
  • Comply with applicable laws, legal processes, or valid government requests.
  • Protect the rights, safety, and security of users and the service.

Third-party service providers may process information under their own privacy policies and applicable agreements. We do not control the independent privacy practices of third-party services.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the application features, fulfill the purposes described in this policy, resolve disputes, maintain security, or comply with legal obligations.

Information stored locally may remain on your device until you delete it, clear application data, or uninstall the application. Information stored through connected services may require a separate deletion request.

For assistance with data deletion, contact us using the email address provided below.

TIMEVORA is not knowingly designed to collect personal information from children in violation of applicable laws. If you believe that a child has provided personal information without appropriate consent, please contact us so that we can review the request and take appropriate action.

9. Your Privacy Choices

You may have the ability to:

  • Disable notifications.
  • Manage usage tracking permissions.

  • Request access to or deletion of information, subject to applicable law.
